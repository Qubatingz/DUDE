DUDE - your AI desktop companion
================================

HOW TO START
1. Right-click the zip you downloaded > Extract All > Extract.
   (Don't run him from inside the zip.)
2. Open the Dude folder and double-click Dude.exe.
3. Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC".
   That happens because Dude is brand new and not signed yet.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. Dude appears at the bottom of your screen, asks your name,
   and asks before he looks at anything.

TALK TO HIM
- Right-click him (or press Ctrl+Shift+D) to open his panel.
- Type in "Talk to Dude", or press the mic button / Ctrl+Shift+Space and speak.
- Try: "find the beat I made last week", "how's my laptop doing?",
  "organize my computer", "what did I work on yesterday?",
  "remind me to call Eric in 20 minutes".

GIVE HIM A REAL AI BRAIN (optional)
Without it he understands simple commands. With it he understands normal sentences.
1. Go to platform.claude.com, sign in, add some credits, and create an API key.
2. Open his panel > Brain tab > paste the key > Connect.
The key stays on this laptop, encrypted. You pay Anthropic per use.

PRIVACY: HE ASKS FIRST
- Access tab: switch on/off what he can see (apps, recent files, memory, folders).
- In folders he only reads file NAMES, sizes and dates. He never deletes or moves anything.
- He goes quiet by himself in full-screen apps, music software and video calls.
- Memory tab > Forget everything wipes his memory.

PLAY
- Grab him and throw him. He walks on top of your open windows.
- His face is in the tray near the clock (click the ^ arrow if you don't see it):
  controls, voice, Studio, check-up, do not disturb, start with Windows, quit.
- Ctrl+Shift+Q makes him leave instantly.

VOICE INPUT NEEDS
- A microphone, and Windows Settings > Privacy > Microphone > let desktop apps use it.
- English speech recognition (Settings > Time & language > Speech).

TO REMOVE HIM
Quit him from the tray, then delete the Dude folder.
His settings live in %APPDATA%\Dude (delete that too for a clean slate).
