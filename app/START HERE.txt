DUDE - your AI desktop companion
================================

HOW TO START
1. Right-click the zip you downloaded > Extract All > Extract.
   (Don't run him from inside the zip.)
2. Open the folder and double-click Dude.exe.
3. Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC".
   That happens because Dude is brand new and not signed yet.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. Dude appears at the bottom of your screen, asks your name,
   and asks before he looks at anything.

ON / OFF
- Click his face in the tray (near the clock) to switch him on or off.
  On: he pops up and listens. Off: he disappears and the mic is off.

TALK TO HIM
- Just say "Dude..." and talk. When he answers or asks you something,
  you can reply without saying his name.
- Or right-click him (Ctrl+Shift+D) and type in his panel.
- His calendar: "Dude, studio session with Eric Saturday at 3".
  "Dude, what's on today?"
- The first time he talks, his new voice downloads (about 130 MB, one time).

HE REMEMBERS AND LEARNS
- "Where's the contract from last month?" "What did I download yesterday?"
  "What does my contract say about payment?" "Who is Eric?"
  (He asks once if he may remember your whole laptop: file names and dates,
  and the text inside your documents. It all stays on your laptop.)
- Tap Learn and give him a PDF or Word file, or paste a link. He summarizes it
  and remembers it: "what does the contract say about payment?"
- Tap Scan: what you can delete, what to move to a drive, what to keep.
  He never deletes anything himself.
- "To do: finish the hook". "My song Amahoro drops on November 20" makes a
  release plan. Every morning he tells you what's next.
- Try: "find the beat I made last week", "how's my laptop doing?",
  "organize my computer", "what did I work on yesterday?",
  "remind me to call Eric in 20 minutes".

HE GETS TO KNOW YOU
- He asks you 4 quick questions (what you're building, what you're learning, your dream).
- He talks FIRST: when you open an app, in the morning, late at night, when you work
  too long, and a random thought every 10-15 minutes. He stays quiet while you focus.
- Every morning he asks for today's mission, and checks on it in the evening.
- English practice: a word of the day, small challenges, better ways to say things.
- Tap the laughing face on his speech bubble when he's funny, or the thumbs down
  when he's not. He learns your sense of humor and never repeats a killed joke.
- Teach tab: change what he talks about, how often, and teach him your own lines
  ("When I open TikTok, say: Ten minutes, bro").
- You can also just tell him: "roast me", "motivate me", "word of the day",
  "I'm overthinking", "talk less", "when I open YouTube, say Tutorial or cats?"
- If you allow "Which app you're using", he learns your routine on this laptop:
  when you start, your busiest hour, the apps and sites you live in, late nights.

STUDIO HELPER (FL Studio)
- He keeps track of all your FL Studio projects, so you never forget a name again.
- "show my beats": a studio report with untitled projects, unfinished beats (lots of
  work, no export), piles of versions, old gems and your favorite BPM.
- "what was I working on?": your latest project and how long you worked on it.
- "find the beat with the log drum": he looks INSIDE projects (samples, instruments,
  pattern names, tempo like "140 bpm").
- When you open FL Studio, he offers the project you were on last.
- When you save an "Untitled" project, he suggests a real name, like "112bpm Log Drum Oct 9".
  He renames only when you click Rename, and "undo" changes it back.
  He never moves or deletes your projects.
- He asks first: for the FL Studio projects folder, and to "Look inside music projects".

TEACH HIM YOURSELF (any language, in your voice)
- Teach tab > "Teach him something": pick WHEN (I say..., I open an app, every morning,
  late at night, I come back, I finish something, anytime), then type what he says,
  or press the mic and say it in YOUR voice. Kinyarwanda, English, anything.
- He can do a move with it too (dab, backflip, noodle dance...).
- Example: when you say "Amakuru?", he answers "Ni meza!" in your voice.
- Or just tell him: "when I say show them, do a backflip", or "learn this".

MY VOICE (he talks like you)
- Brain tab > His voice > My voice, then read the card out loud (about 15 seconds).
- The first time he downloads his new vocal cords (about 160 MB, one time only).
- It all happens on this laptop. Free, and your recording never leaves it.
- He needs a second or two before he talks. The Windows voice is instant.
- "Delete my recording" in the same place removes it.

GIVE HIM A REAL AI BRAIN (optional)
Without it he understands simple commands. With it he understands normal sentences,
and his first-to-speak thoughts are written fresh for you, using what he knows about you.
1. Go to platform.claude.com, sign in, add some credits, and create an API key.
2. Open his panel > Brain tab > paste the key > Connect.
The key stays on this laptop, encrypted. You pay Anthropic per use.

PRIVACY: HE ASKS FIRST
- Access tab: switch on/off what he can see (apps, recent files, memory, folders).
- In folders he only reads file NAMES, sizes and dates (and, if you allow it, what's inside
  your FL Studio projects). He never deletes or moves anything. He renames a project only
  when you click Rename.
- He goes quiet by himself in full-screen apps, music software and video calls.
- Memory tab > Forget everything wipes his memory, his routine notes, what you taught him
  and your voice recordings.

PLAY
- Grab him and throw him. He walks on top of your open windows.
- His face is in the tray near the clock (click the ^ arrow if you don't see it):
  controls, voice, Studio, check-up, do not disturb, start with Windows, quit.
- Ctrl+Shift+Q makes him leave instantly.

VOICE INPUT NEEDS
- A microphone, and Windows Settings > Privacy > Microphone > let desktop apps use it.
- English speech recognition (Settings > Time & language > Speech).

TO REMOVE HIM
Quit him from the tray, then delete the Dude folder.
His settings live in %APPDATA%\Dude (delete that too for a clean slate).
